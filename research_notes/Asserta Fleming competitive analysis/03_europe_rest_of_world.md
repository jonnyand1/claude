# Asserta (Fleming / Silicon) – International (non-Spain) Footprint

Research date: 2 Oct 2026. Labels: CONFIRMED / CONFIRMED (company claim) / INDICATIVE / UNKNOWN. Method: asserta.net pages pulled raw (HTML, incl. the Leaflet map code and logo files on the Experiences page), Medilogy brochure PDF text-extracted, TED API queried, web searches in EN/IT/ES/PT. Tool budget was limited (~25 calls); many national portals could not be queried (see Gaps / Blocked sources).

## Q1. Headline footprint and country-by-country table

### Takeaway
Asserta says it has worked in "20 countries", and the map on its own website pins 17 countries besides Spain. However, the only non-Spain **commercial deployment evidence** is client logos and press quotes. They point to Latin America (Mexico, Colombia, Peru, Chile) and the Middle East (Bahrain, through Indra's iSeha programme). Most European pins (UK, Germany, Czech Republic, Ireland, Greece, Italy) appear to be **EU research-consortium partners, not customers**. Italy is the only country with a **named reseller/distributor (Medilogy S.r.l.)**, and no Italian Fleming customer was found. No foreign subsidiary was found.

### Inferences
- Fleming's stated installed base (14 hospitals, 600+ PHC centres, "Spain and Latin America") suggests that any LatAm Fleming customers sit among the Colombian/Peruvian/Mexican/Chilean logos. The 600+ PHC figure plausibly comes mostly from Spain, e.g. ICS/SERGAS; this is an inference.
- Before the April 2025 Silicon deal, Asserta was a ~12-person firm. Its "20 countries" claim mixes research-consortium partner countries with consulting/analytics projects. It should not be read as 20 countries with software customers.
- Bahrain work was most likely delivered under Indra (Spanish IT prime) on the iSeha national HIS. This fits the Alliances page wording "structured subcontracting" and "joint bids and UTE models" (inference).

### Gaps
- No contract, tender award or purchase order for Asserta/Fleming/Silicon was found outside Spain.
- The product delivered to each LatAm/Middle East logo client is not stated anywhere.

## Q2. Italy – Medilogy and Italian customers

### Takeaway
Medilogy S.r.l. (Milan) is an Italian clinical decision-support vendor whose main product is MediDSS (an Italian adaptation of Duodecim's EBMeDS). It distributes Fleming PROA in Italy under an Italian-language brochure from around April 2025. No Italian hospital customer, tender or MEPA listing for Fleming was found. No evidence was found that Grifols Silicon was ever installed in Italy.

## Q3. Grifols Silicon – historical footprint and the transaction

### Takeaway
Grifols launched Silicon in 2006 as a hospital pharmacy management / e-prescribing system. Public sources only document Spanish installations. Grifols' hospital-logistics business had Latin American (Kardex distribution) and Portuguese/Chilean automation activity, but no source ties Silicon itself to a non-Spanish install. The sale to Asserta was small: 7 employees were transferred on 1 April 2025, and no price was disclosed.

### Cited Findings
- Silicon was released by Grifols in 2006. It runs on Unix and uses web technology for e-prescription and hospital pharmacy management — CONFIRMED — [Grifols: Automating hospital pharmacy services](https://www.grifols.com/en/-/automating-hospital-pharmacy-services)
- Silicon and Synfhos were part of Grifols' Logister software line for hospital pharmacy — CONFIRMED — [Grifols: Logister](https://grifols.com/-/logister)
- Silicon is listed in the SEFH CETEC (Spanish Society of Hospital Pharmacy) technology catalogue. The summary named Spanish reference hospitals, which I did not verify verbatim — INDICATIVE — [CETEC SEFH](https://cetec.sefh.es/producto/silicon/?lang=en)
- Search-result snippet: Grifols hospital-automation material mentions "more than 100 automation projects ... in Spain, Portugal and Chile". This refers to automation (Kardex/Pyxis/Misterium), not explicitly Silicon — INDICATIVE — [Grifols automation page](https://www.grifols.com/-/automating-hospital-pharmacy-services)
- 2006: Grifols signed a Kardex Remstar distribution agreement for the main Latin American countries — CONFIRMED — [Grifols press release 2006](https://www.grifols.com/documents/3627767/3632465/05_np_19062006_signs_agreement_kardex_latin_america_en.pdf)
- Transaction: Grifols sold the "unitat de negoci del producte Silicon" to Asserta Global Healthcare Solutions, effective **1 April 2025**. **7 employees** were transferred under art. 44 ET (Spanish employment law) with a €7,800 one-off payment each. No price or countries were stated — CONFIRMED — [CCOO Indústria](https://www.ccoo.cat/industria/noticies/ccoo-aconsegueix-una-compensacio-per-a-la-plantilla-de-grifols-afectada-per-la-subrogacio-a-asserta-que-ha-comprat-la-unitat-de-negoci-del-producte-silicon/)
- Grifols' Form 6-K filed Feb 2025 (FY2024 results/annual report) contains no occurrence of "Asserta" or "Silicon". This was checked by full-text scan of the filing, so no price was disclosed there — CONFIRMED (absence) — [SEC 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1438569/000110465925017501/tm257688d1_6k.htm)
- Asserta now runs a "Silicon Division" (GM Juan José Ibáñez del Saz; Head of Development & Support Jose Pineda Sánchez; two "Regional Manager TS" roles: Víctor Naranjo and Miguel Ángel González Prieto, the latter from University of Vigo). Every profile points to Spanish regions, not foreign markets — CONFIRMED (company claim) — [Asserta team](https://asserta.net/en/team/)
- Silicon NextGen is now marketed as integrating with "National prescription platforms" and deployable "as part of national or multicenter programs". No non-Spanish installs are named — CONFIRMED (company claim) — [Silicon NextGen](https://asserta.net/en/silicon-nextgen/)

### Inferences
- Seven employees, no disclosed price, and nothing in Grifols' annual filing indicate a small, immaterial carve-out of a mainly Spanish installed base. The new "Europe" logos added in June 2026 (SERGAS hospitals, ICS, Tortosa, Viladecans, etc.) look like the inherited Silicon customer list, and all of them are Spanish (inference from upload dates 2026/06 on logo files).

### Gaps
- Grifols annual reports 2018–2023 were not scanned for Silicon (budget). The Grifols Silicon product page (grifols.com/es/product/-/product/spain/silicon) was not fetched.
- Whether Silicon was ever installed in Portugal, Chile or Mexico is UNKNOWN.

## Q4. Legal presence, partnerships, research consortia, frameworks

### Takeaway
No foreign subsidiary, branch or named distributor exists on asserta.net. Medilogy (Italy) is the only identified distributor, and Indra (Bahrain) the only identified prime-contractor partner. Asserta's documented EU research participation is in EU Health Programme / H2020 projects, not procurement frameworks. No framework agreement membership was found in any country.

  - CONFIRMED (company claim): [MyHealth](https://asserta.net/reasearch-projects/myhealth/), [Share4Rare](https://asserta.net/reasearch-projects/share4rare/), [CLOSER](https://asserta.net/reasearch-projects/closer/)
