# Asserta in Spain: Fleming / Fleming-PROA installed base, public contracts, and update on the Silicon footprint

Research cut-off: 2 October 2026. Evidence labels: CONFIRMED (independent public source) / CONFIRMED (company claim) / CONFIRMED (user-provided internal intelligence) / INDICATIVE (with reasoning) / UNKNOWN (with where searched).

Overall headline: **No Spanish public contract, tender, award or named hospital for Fleming / Fleming-PROA was found.** The only Fleming installed-base figure is Asserta's own claim of "600+ primary care centres and 14 hospitals in Spain and Latin America" (March 2024), with no site named. The Silicon footprint, by contrast, is well documented. The new Silicon-adjacent items found here are: (a) a SERGAS hospital-pharmacy drug catalogue contract worth €453k, the largest Asserta-direct award found; (b) a correction to the Huelva item, which is Hospital Infanta Elena with Silicon® named explicitly; (c) Reus SSJRBC 0358/25 was in fact awarded to Asserta, and its specifications require a PROA/antibiotic-optimisation module. That is the only public tender found that combines pharmacy analytics with PROA.

## 1. Which Spanish hospitals use or contract Fleming / Fleming-PROA? Where did it originate, and how many sites does Asserta claim?

### Takeaway
No hospital or health service in Spain is publicly named as a Fleming user. I found no tender, award, minor contract or memoria that mentions "Fleming" with Asserta on the aggregators reviewed (el-vinculo, gobierto, menjometre, sociedad.info) or on Asserta's own pages. Asserta claims 600+ primary care centres and 14 hospitals across Spain and Latin America (CONFIRMED, company claim, 2024). Which hospital first deployed Fleming is UNKNOWN.
