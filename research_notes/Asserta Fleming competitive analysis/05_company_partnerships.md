# Asserta Global Healthcare Solutions SL: company profile, financials, headcount, ownership and partnerships

Research date: 2 Oct 2026. Languages searched: ES / CA / EN. Labels: CONFIRMED = independent/registry source; CONFIRMED (company claim) = asserta.net or company-authored exhibitor text; INDICATIVE = inference with reasoning; UNKNOWN = searched, not found.

## 1. Legal identity, registry data, directors, ownership

### Takeaway
Asserta Global Healthcare Solutions SL (CIF B65880593) is a small, founder-run Spanish limited company. It was incorporated in October 2012 and is headquartered in **Sant Quirze del Vallès (Barcelona province)**, not Barcelona city. Founder Josep Monterde Junyent is the sole administrator. No investor, VC, PE or Grifols shareholding was found anywhere.

## 2. Financials (filed accounts via aggregators)

### Takeaway
Exact figures are paywalled. What the public tiers show: the sales band is **€1.5–3 M**, sales grew **+148% in 2025 vs 2024** (the first year with Silicon), total assets rose +133%, and profit was slightly positive (economic ROA 1.2%, ROE 4.09%). The 2025 accounts were filed on 31 Aug 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Sales range €1.5–3 M. RANGE, not exact. [empresite](https://empresite.eleconomista.es/ASSERTA-GLOBAL-HEALTHCARE-SOLUTIONS.html) shows this alongside 2025 employee data, so it most likely refers to 2025.
- 2025 sales up 148.08% vs 2024; total assets up 133.34% from 2024 to 2025; economic return 1.2% and financial return 4.09% (2025), so the company was profitable but on thin margins. The latest accounts are FY2025, filed 31 Aug 2026. CONFIRMED (aggregator): [einforma](https://www.einforma.com/informacion-empresa/asserta-global-healthcare-solutions).
- infonif masks revenue, EBITDA, net profit and employee values for non-premium users: [infonif](https://infonif.economia3.com/ficha-empresa/asserta-global-healthcare-solutions-sl).
- Empresite ranking: national #188,622; Barcelona province #28,892; sector #1,484. It says the ranking fell in 2025, which is odd given +148% sales and may reflect a ranking-method artefact: [empresite](https://empresite.eleconomista.es/ASSERTA-GLOBAL-HEALTHCARE-SOLUTIONS.html).

### Inferences
- INDICATIVE: if 2025 sales are in the €1.5–3 M band and grew +148%, then 2024 sales were roughly €0.6–1.2 M. Method: band ÷ 2.48. The roughly €1–1.8 M uplift is consistent with 9 months of Silicon maintenance revenue (April–December 2025). This is an estimate, not a filed figure.

### Gaps
- Exact revenue, net profit and EBITDA for any year: paywalled (einforma, infonif premium, axesor). Earlier years (2013–2024) were not visible.

## 4. Grifols → Asserta Silicon deal

### Takeaway
Asserta bought Grifols' Silicon business unit (Silicon is an e-prescribing and hospital pharmacy management system launched by Grifols in 2006). It took effect on **1 April 2025**, with 7 employees subrogated. The price is **not disclosed**. No Grifols press release was found, and Grifols' 2025 20-F does not mention it, which implies it was immaterial to Grifols.

### Cited Findings
- Scope: the "unitat de negoci del producte Silicon", with staff subrogated from 1 April 2025: [CCOO](https://www.ccoo.cat/industria/noticies/ccoo-aconsegueix-una-compensacio-per-a-la-plantilla-de-grifols-afectada-per-la-subrogacio-a-asserta-que-ha-comprat-la-unitat-de-negoci-del-producte-silicon/).
- Silicon was released by Grifols in 2006 and covers prescription, transcription and validation, compounding, dispensing, administration (eMAR) and logistics: [managementmedication.com](https://www.managementmedication.com/en/spain/pharmacy-information-systems-epoee/silicon); [SEFH CETEC catalogue](https://cetec.sefh.es/producto/silicon/?lang=en). **The SEFH CETEC catalogue still lists Grifols as the supplier**, so it is stale.
- Asserta now markets the product as "Silicon NextGen" ("The Operational Core of Integrated Pharmacotherapy Governance") and has a "Silicon Division": [asserta.net](https://asserta.net/); [team](https://asserta.net/team/).
- Grifols FY2025 20-F: 0 hits for "Asserta" and 0 for "Silicon". The hospital automation business stays in Kiro Grifols S.L., which is still a separate CGU: [SEC 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1438569/000110465926044901/grfs-20251231x20f.htm).
- Price: UNKNOWN. I searched Grifols 6-K/20-F, Spanish press (ES/CA) and CCOO.

### Inferences
- INDICATIVE: Grifols kept Kiro (robotics) and divested only the pharmacy software. Whether a Silicon–Kiro interoperability or commercial agreement exists after the deal is UNKNOWN, but it is likely needed for the installed base.

### Gaps
- Deal price, earn-outs, transitional service agreement, number of Silicon customer contracts transferred, and whether the Grifols Movaco legal entity was the seller (not confirmed by any source found).

## 5. Leadership and background

### Takeaway
The company is led by a veteran hospital pharmacist (founder and CEO Josep Monterde, ex-Vall d'Hebron pharmacy director). The Silicon leaders have long healthcare-software careers, and some are presumably ex-Grifols.

### Cited Findings
- Josep Monterde: PhD in Pharmacy (UB) and hospital pharmacy specialist. Director of Central Clinical Services and Pharmacy, Vall d'Hebron University Hospital (14 years); Head of Pharmaceutical Services at IMAS, Barcelona municipal hospitals (9 years); Director of Pharmaceutical Care at the 1992 Barcelona Olympics/Paralympics; associate professor at UB; faculty at Universidad de la República (Uruguay); 8 years as Vice-President of the Acadèmia de Ciències Mèdiques de Catalunya i Balears; advisor to the Spanish Ministry of Health's DG Farmacia; coordinator of the EPIMED study; about 100 publications. CONFIRMED (company claim): [asserta.net/team/josep-monterde](https://asserta.net/team/josep-monterde/).

  - Pineda (Head Dev & Support Silicon): 20+ years in clinical software.
  - Elena Tomás: hospital pharmacist, master's in biostatistics.
  - Julio Martínez: Clinical Advisor, 17+ years managing hospital pharmacy.
