# Publications and evidence on Ascentry (formerly BYG4lab / BYG Informatique; includes Partner4lab / Info Partner and Finbiosoft) products

Research cut-off: 2026-09-26. The reader is a bioMérieux LUMED BDM. No internal LUMED intelligence was used.

Product lineage used for the searches (from company sources):
- Info Partner (Vandoeuvre/Villers-lès-Nancy), later branded **Partner4lab**. The company says it was "Editeur et concepteur des solutions BACTERIO – VIGI@ct – VIGIguard – INFECTIO". ## Q1. Papers where hospitals used BYG4lab / Ascentry (or predecessor) software for HAI surveillance, epidemiology, BMR/BHRe alerts or AMS

### Takeaway
I found no peer-reviewed paper that evaluates Ynfectio or Infection Tracker (or Infectio / InfectioGlobal) as an intervention, with outcomes on HAI, outbreak detection or AMS. The IPC evidence is limited to four French pharmacy or biology theses (grey literature, independent, descriptive) that name the software as the lab epidemiology or data-extraction tool. Only one of them (Reims 2025) links it to a BMR alert workflow (MRSA alert form sent to the EOH). A cluster of 2007–2020 peer-reviewed papers (Italy, Portugal, France) uses **VIGI@ct / VIGIguard**, sold under the **bioMérieux** name. Ascentry/BYG4lab says Info Partner designed and published these tools. For a bioMérieux reader, that makes the Infectio line historically a former bioMérieux-distributed product family.

### Inferences
- The independent evidence that Ynfectio/Infection Tracker works for IPC is essentially nil. The theses show installed-base presence: CHU Reims (InfectioGlobal V4, 2025), CHRU Nancy (InfectioGlobal, 2020; Bacterio/Entrepot earlier) and CH Abbeville (Infectio.Global, 2019). They also show usage as an alert or data-extraction layer, not as a validated CDSS. This supports framing Ascentry as a lab-centric epidemiology tool with a thin evidence base.
- At Nancy, even with InfectioGlobal in place, the EOH built its own Access database for BHRe contact management. That suggests the Infectio product then lacked adequate contact-tracing functions. Ascentry now claims contact-readmission alerts.
- The VIGI@ct/VIGIguard papers are the most substantial peer-reviewed body of evidence for this product lineage. They are branded bioMérieux, which matters for competitive messaging. bioMérieux may hold its own historical records on the Info Partner OEM or distribution relationship. Verify internally before using this externally.
- Ménard (CHU Rennes) publishes actively on IPC and outbreaks: NDM (ARIC 2026), ESBL quantitative antibiogram (JHI 2023). His participation in the sponsored SF2H 2025 session suggests Rennes is a Ynfectio user or pilot site. His NDM paper (PMC13466148) does not mention Ynfectio and declares no competing interests. INDICATIVE.
