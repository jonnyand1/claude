# Ascentry (formerly BYG4lab): corporate profile, events and partnerships

Research cut-off: 2026-09-26. About 25 tool calls. Each claim is labelled: CONFIRMED (official/independent), CONFIRMED (company claim), INDICATIVE (inference, reasoning given), UNKNOWN (searched, not found).

## Basics: legal entities, HQ, founding, founders, and the rebrand

### Takeaway
Ascentry is the trading brand of a French group based in L'Union, near Toulouse. BYG was founded in 1982. Cyril Verhille acquired it in 2012. The brand changed to Ascentry at ADLM in Chicago in July 2025. The rebrand was not caused by a new deal. It unified BYG4lab and Finbiosoft (a Finnish company acquired in March 2024) under one name, two years after Keensight Capital's 2022 majority buyout. The legal entity names changed later: the holding company was renamed from "BYG4lab Group" to "ASCENTRY GROUP" on 16/07/2026 according to Pappers.
