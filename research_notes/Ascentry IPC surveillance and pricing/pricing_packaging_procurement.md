## Q0. Identity and rebrand (context for every procurement search)

### Takeaway
BYG4lab became Ascentry at ADLM 2025, after merging with Finbiosoft (Finland). The French operating company, BYG INFORMATIQUE (SIREN 326 649 407), is now registered as ASCENTRY FRANCE. ## Q1. How does Ascentry price its software?

### Takeaway
There is no public price list. The procurement evidence shows the classic French hospital model for lab software: the software is acquired once (licence, installation and connections), then maintenance is paid under multi-year contracts, usually 4 years and often awarded without competition because only the publisher can maintain the software. Maintenance is priced per product and per site/GHT establishment, and middleware maintenance covers "connexions d'automates", i.e. per analyser connection.

The one sizeable, published number is **€415,155 over 4 years (≈€104k/year)** at CHU Caen Normandie. This covers corrective and evolutive maintenance for four products (NYNA middleware, the Ynfectio epidemiology/hygiene software, Qualynk and EVM-IH) across three GHT establishments. It is not broken down by product.

There is no evidence of per-bed or per-test pricing. The cloud/SaaS subscription model is confirmed only for the Finbiosoft line (Validation Manager, "cloud-based"). For Infection Tracker it is UNKNOWN.

### Inferences
- **Maintenance cost of the epidemiology module on its own.** INDICATIVE: Nevers pays about €2.6k/year for Infectio.Labo/Ynfectio Labo maintenance and updates at one hospital. Caen pays about €104k/year for four products across three GHT sites. If maintenance runs at the usual 15–20% of licence value (a market convention, not sourced for Ascentry), Nevers' implied Infectio.Labo licence would be in the low tens of thousands of euros. That is a small-hospital price point far below a CDSS enterprise deal. This is a heuristic estimate only.
- **Pricing drivers.** INDICATIVE: Several contracts itemise "maintenance du matériel, du logiciel et de la connexion d'automates", which points to per-connection (per-analyser) pricing for middleware. The Caen notice covers "trois établissements du GHT", which points to per-site/per-establishment scope. No evidence of per-bed or per-test pricing was found.
- **Procurement route.** INDICATIVE: Most maintenance renewals are single-source (no competition), and many are below EU thresholds. Once installed, Ascentry products lock the customer in through exclusive maintenance. First-time acquisition of Ynfectio as a separate competitive lot was not found.
- **Relevance to bioMérieux.** INDICATIVE: In Belgium and France, BYG4lab connectivity is often specified inside IVD instrument tenders (MALDI, AST, immunohaematology). In those cases the IVD vendor (including bioMérieux at HUmani) bears or passes through the connection cost. This is a channel relationship bioMérieux already has with Ascentry.
- **Market comparison.** INDICATIVE (no source found comparing French lab-software vendors' pricing): The pattern of licence plus about 4-year maintenance contracts, awarded by negotiated or no-competition procedure, matches the DECP entries above. It appears to be the norm for installed hospital lab software in France. SaaS subscription is still the exception in French public hospitals.

## Q2. Are the epidemiology/IPC modules sold standalone (on a third-party LIS) or only as an add-on?

### Takeaway
The premise of the question needs correcting. BYG4lab/Ascentry is historically a middleware and data-management vendor, not a LIS vendor. Its only LIS-like tie is an OEM/partner middleware (TD-Mind) with Technidata's LIS. So its epidemiology product necessarily sits on top of someone else's LIS.

The design is LIS-agnostic:
- INFECTIO.GLOBAL collects data from the LIS "via a HL7 connection".
- INFECTIO.LABO connects to the lab's instruments.
- Ynfectio is described as connected to both the HIS and the LIS.

The procurement evidence (Nevers, Caen) is ambiguous. At Nevers, Infectio Labo has its own maintenance contract, which suggests a standalone product line. At Caen, Ynfectio is bundled with the Ascentry middleware in one contract. That fits a cross-sell into the installed middleware base. **This cannot be definitively answered from public sources.**

### Cited Findings
- CONFIRMED (company, via search snippets of the Partner4lab product pages): INFECTIO.GLOBAL is a "complete epidemiology platform" for hospitals that "collects data from the Laboratory Information System (LIS) via a HL7 connection". INFECTIO.LABO is "connected to laboratory instruments". The partner4lab.com pages were not directly fetchable (DNS failure), so this relies on search-result text. — [INFECTIO.GLOBAL](https://partner4lab.com/shop/infectio-global/); [INFECTIO.LABO](https://partner4lab.com/fr/shop/infectio-labo/)
- CONFIRMED (trade press / company): Ynfectio (2023) is "connecté aux SIH et SIL". It includes a hygiene module for the équipes opérationnelles d'hygiène (EOH), real-time alerts on BHRe, resistance phenotypes and notifiable diseases, and management of healthcare-associated infections (IAS). — [Biologiste365](https://www.biologiste365.fr/informations-produits/surveillance-epidemiologique/)
- CONFIRMED (company): Infection Tracker offers "native integration with laboratory and hospital systems" and "90+ sites across Europe". For stewardship it offers only resistance-trend insight for prescribing and automated antibiograms, with no prescription-level AMS workflow described. — [Ascentry Infection Tracker](https://www.ascentry.com/products/infection-tracker/)
- CONFIRMED (official): CH Nevers holds a separate 4-year contract for the epidemiology product alone ("INFECTIO LABO / YNFECTIOLABO", €10,315). — [Pappers DECP listing](https://www.pappers.fr/entreprise/byg-informatique-326649407)
- CONFIRMED (official): CHU Caen buys epidemiology/hygiene (Ynfectio) maintenance together with the NYNA middleware, Qualynk and EVM-IH in one contract. — [TED 348571-2024](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/348571-2024)
- CONFIRMED (press): Technidata (the LIS vendor TDNexLabs, claimed ~50% of French hospital groups) partnered with BYG4lab in October 2021 to create the TD-Mind middleware on BYG4lab technology. This is an OEM/partner channel alongside a third-party LIS. — [Inovallée](https://www.inovallee.com/technidata-tisse-un-partenariat-strategique-avec-byg4lab-pour-repondre-aux-enjeux-futurs-des-laboratoires-medicaux/)

### Inferences
- INDICATIVE: Because Ascentry sells no mainstream LIS, every Ynfectio or Infection Tracker site necessarily runs a third-party LIS, so in that sense it is "standalone on a third-party LIS". The open commercial question is different: is it mostly sold to existing Ascentry middleware customers (cross-sell), or also to labs with no Ascentry middleware (true standalone)?
- INDICATIVE: The legacy Partner4lab base (Infectio) was built independently of BYG middleware, since Partner4lab was a separate company before 2020. This suggests at least part of the 90+ sites are standalone epidemiology customers. Nevers' separate contract is consistent with this.
- INDICATIVE, with low confidence: the HL7 LIS feed plus HIS connection design means the product can be deployed without Ascentry middleware. The integration cost falls on HL7 interfaces charged by the LIS vendor.

### Gaps
Evidence that would settle the question:
- The Nevers and Caen CCTPs, showing whether a BYG middleware is present at Nevers.
- A list of Infection Tracker/Ynfectio reference sites cross-referenced with their LIS and middleware vendors. The ANS/Ségur SIL vendor listings could supply the LIS side.
- Any tender where the epidemiology software is a separate lot with a competitive first acquisition. None was found on TED, and BOAMP was not queried directly.
