# Ascentry (ex-BYG4lab): footprint outside France (Europe and rest of world)

Research cut-off: 2026-09-26. Labels: CONFIRMED (independent/official), CONFIRMED (company claim), INDICATIVE (inference, reasoning stated), UNKNOWN (searched, not found; places searched named).
Scope limit: about 30 tool calls. No procurement portal (TED, simap.ch, publicprocurement.be, PLACSP, Find a Tender, Contracts Finder) was queried directly. They were reached only through general web search, so "no contract found" means **unverified, not absent**.

## Q0. Identity, home market and legal entities

### Takeaway
Ascentry is the name BYG4lab (legal entity BYG Informatique SAS, L'Union near Toulouse, France) has used since July 2025. It is French. The only subsidiaries it names are in the **United States** and **Finland** (via Finbiosoft). No Belgian or Swiss entity turned up.

### Cited Findings
- CONFIRMED (independent): BYG4lab renamed itself Ascentry, with the announcement at ADLM 2025 in Chicago (28–30 July 2025). Product renames: nYna became Ascentry Lab Composer, Ypoc became POC Controller, **Ynfectio became Ascentry Infection Tracker**, Validation Manager became Ascentry Validation Manager. — [Biologiste365](https://www.biologiste365.fr/biologiste365-fr/actualites-biologiste365-fr/byg4lab-devient-ascentry/); [Newswise press release, 28 Jul 2025](https://www.newswise.com/articles/ascentry-launches-at-adlm-2025-ushering-in-a-new-era-of-laboratory-software)
- CONFIRMED (company claim): founded in France in 1982 as BYG; about 120 employees worldwide; "4,500+ labs served worldwide"; acquired Info Partner in 2020 ("microbiology, epidemiology, and infection control"); acquired Finbiosoft and created a US subsidiary in 2024–25. — [Ascentry About us](https://www.ascentry.com/about-us/)
- CONFIRMED (company claim, association directory): "plus de 120 collaborateurs"; "leader français avec également une forte implantation à l'internationale"; "une filiale aux Etats-Unis et en Finlande"; solutions available in 11 languages; used by 4,500+ labs. — [French Healthcare Association member page](https://frenchhealthcare.fr/en/adherents/byg4lab/)
- CONFIRMED (independent): the registered French entity is BYG INFORMATIQUE, SIREN 326649407, L'Union (31240). — [Pappers](https://www.pappers.fr/entreprise/byg-informatique-326649407); [Societe.com](https://www.societe.com/societe/byg-informatique-326649407.html)
- CONFIRMED (independent): BYG4lab agreed to acquire Finbiosoft (founded 2011; Validation Manager) on 14 March 2024, backed by Keensight Capital. Finbiosoft's network then spanned "16 countries". — [Keensight Capital press release](https://keensight.com/byg4lab-to-acquire-finbiosoft-a-leading-software-provider-for-medical-laboratories-with-the-support-of-keensight-capital/)
- CONFIRMED (company claim): the US contact is a "VP Sales US" and there is a US toll-free number. BYG4lab, Inc. exhibited at ADLM 2025. — [Newswise](https://www.newswise.com/articles/ascentry-launches-at-adlm-2025-ushering-in-a-new-era-of-laboratory-software); [ADLM 2025 exhibitor listing](https://adlm25.myexpoonline.com/co/byg4lab-inc)

### Inferences
- INDICATIVE: the Finnish subsidiary is almost certainly the former Finbiosoft Oy, whose product is Validation Manager. The Finland entity and the Nordic/UK/German reference customers therefore come from the **Validation Manager** business, not the IPC/epidemiology line.

### Gaps
- UNKNOWN: whether there are legal entities in Belgium (KBO/BCE), Switzerland (Zefix/moneyhouse) or the UK (Companies House). None of these registers were queried directly, and no web source mentioned such entities. **Manual check needed.**

## Q1. Belgium

### Takeaway
Belgium is covered **through the Info Partner / partner4lab legacy**, which is the IPC/epidemiology line. Partner4lab said it had installations in Belgium, and a named Belgian site in **Arlon** ran PILOT.4lab microbiology middleware plus the **INFECTIO.GLOBAL epidemiology** solution. That product is the predecessor of Ynfectio / Infection Tracker. No Belgian public contract, entity or distributor was found.

### Cited Findings
- CONFIRMED (company claim, pre-acquisition): partner4lab (brand of INFO PARTNER, founded 1989, publisher of VIGI@ct and VIGIguard) "has equipped hundreds of private and hospital laboratories in France, Belgium, Luxembourg, Switzerland, Great-Britain and many other countries." — [partner4lab Company page](https://partner4lab.com/en/company/). I saw this only as a search snippet; the site did not resolve (DNS failure) when fetched.
- CONFIRMED (company claim): a technical platform in **Arlon, Belgium** runs PILOT.4lab paperless and is "equipped with PILOT.4lab microbiology middleware including Exact manager, I.MALDI and the INFECTIO.GLOBAL epidemiology solution". — [partner4lab, feedback from the first PILOT.4lab user day](https://partner4lab.com/feed-back-journee-pilot4lab/). Search snippet only; the page could not be fetched.
- CONFIRMED (company claim): INFECTIO.GLOBAL is "a complete epidemiology platform connected via HL7 link to the Laboratory Information System, designed to monitor and detect particular events". — [partner4lab INFECTIO.GLOBAL](https://partner4lab.com/shop/infectio-global/)
- CONFIRMED (company claim): BYG Informatique acquired Info Partner/Partner4lab in 2020. The legacy Infectio software was rebuilt on the YLine platform as **Ynfectio**, launched in France in 2023. — [Biologiste365](https://www.biologiste365.fr/strategie/numerique/quand-les-donnees-de-biologie-ne-sont-quun-depart/); [Ascentry About us](https://www.ascentry.com/about-us/)
- CONFIRMED (company claim): Ascentry's news and events page lists an event in **Brussels**. — [Ascentry news/events](https://www.ascentry.com/case-studies/1ere-annee-dune-fusion-reussie-entre-byg-informatique-et-info-partner-partner4lab/)

### Inferences
- INDICATIVE: the Arlon platform is probably the laboratory of Vivalia (Clinique du Sud-Luxembourg / CSL Arlon). The source only says "Arlon", so this attribution is **unverified**.
- INDICATIVE: Belgian references are most likely francophone Wallonia sites inherited from Info Partner, which was based near Nancy, close to the Belgian and Luxembourg border. I found no Dutch-language (Flemish) references.
- INDICATIVE: whether those sites have moved from INFECTIO.GLOBAL to Ynfectio / Infection Tracker is not documented.

### Gaps
- UNKNOWN: Belgian customers other than Arlon, any contracts on publicprocurement.be or TED, notice IDs or values. Searched by general web search in French, Dutch and English; the portals themselves were not queried.
- UNKNOWN: any link to Sciensano/NSIH surveillance (feeds or national reporting). I found no source connecting Ascentry or partner4lab to Sciensano.
- UNKNOWN: a Belgian entity (KBO not queried) or a local distributor or partner.

## Q3. Other European countries

### Takeaway
Outside France, the documented European footprint consists mainly of **Validation Manager (ex-Finbiosoft)** customers: UK NHS, Germany, the Nordics, Ireland and Estonia. None of these is shown to use the IPC module. The company says Infection Tracker runs at "90+ sites across Europe" but does not break this down by country.

### Cited Findings
- CONFIRMED (company claim): Infection Tracker has "25+ years of expertise" and "90+ sites across Europe". It is developed by Ascentry France under ISO 13485:2016. — [Ascentry Infection Tracker](https://www.ascentry.com/products/infection-tracker/)
- CONFIRMED (company claim): the customer stories are all for Validation Manager. They cover Airedale NHS FT and Bedfordshire Hospitals NHS FT (UK); Labor Dr. Heidrich & Kollegen MVZ and University Hospital Ulm (Germany); Princess Elizabeth Hospital (Guernsey); Cork University Hospital (Ireland); HUSLAB (Finland); Sørlandet Hospital (Norway); Tartu University Hospital (Estonia); Linköping University Hospital (Sweden). Logos also include SYNLAB Group, Unilabs, Thermo Fisher, Finnish Red Cross Blood Service, NHS Lothian and University Hospitals Birmingham, "across more than 18 countries". — [Ascentry customer stories](https://www.ascentry.com/customer-stories/)
- CONFIRMED (company claim): Labor Mönchengladbach (Germany) and SYNLAB Group use Validation Manager. The events page lists events in Aarhus (Denmark), **Bilbao (Spain)**, Brussels, Munich, **Rimini (Italy)**, **Dubai (UAE)** and US cities. — [Ascentry news/events](https://www.ascentry.com/case-studies/1ere-annee-dune-fusion-reussie-entre-byg-informatique-et-info-partner-partner4lab/)
- CONFIRMED (company claim, legacy): partner4lab claimed installations in **Luxembourg** and **Great Britain**. — [partner4lab Company page](https://partner4lab.com/en/company/)
- CONFIRMED (independent): VIGI@ct, the Info Partner product distributed under the **bioMérieux** name, was used at Tor Vergata Teaching Hospital in Rome (**Italy**) for outbreak detection, as published in a peer-reviewed article. This is historical, not a current contract. — [PubMed 17544166](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17544166); [BMC Infect Dis 2008](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1471-2334-8-79)

### Inferences
- INDICATIVE (relevant for LUMED): VIGI@ct was once sold as a bioMérieux product. Ascentry's epidemiology line and its legacy installed base may therefore include former bioMérieux VIGI@ct sites in France, Italy and Belgium. bioMérieux's internal records could confirm this; I could not verify it publicly.
- INDICATIVE: Italy, Spain and the UAE appear only as trade-show attendance (Rimini is probably the SIBioC/SIMeL congress, Dubai probably Medlab Middle East), which suggests selling or research activity rather than known contracts.

### Gaps
- UNKNOWN: contracts in Spain (PLACSP not queried), UK (Find a Tender and Contracts Finder not queried; Validation Manager purchases by NHS trusts are likely but no notice found), Germany, the Netherlands (no evidence at all), Portugal (no evidence at all) and Italy (no current tender found).

## Q4. Rest of world

### Takeaway
The **USA** is the only non-European market with a legal entity. A **QuidelOrtho** OEM/software-development partnership provides global reach for the middleware. I found nothing sourced for the Maghreb, francophone Africa, Asia-Pacific or French overseas territories.

### Cited Findings
- CONFIRMED (independent/partner): in July 2023 QuidelOrtho entered a software development partnership with BYG4lab to strengthen its informatics offerings. The deal extended an existing agreement between the two companies covering France. — [QuidelOrtho press release](https://www.quidelortho.com/global/en/resources/press-releases/quidelortho-partners-with-bytg4lab-to-strengthen-informatics-offerings); [Business Wire, 24 Jul 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230724368435/en/QuidelOrtho-Partners-With-BYG4lab-to-Strengthen-Informatics-Offerings)
- CONFIRMED (company claim): BYG4lab is the "privileged partner of several companies in the IVD industry including instrument suppliers and both private and public medical laboratory networks". Its first IVD-supplier partnership in France dates from 2000. — [French Healthcare Association](https://frenchhealthcare.fr/en/adherents/byg4lab/); [Ascentry About us](https://www.ascentry.com/about-us/)
- CONFIRMED (company claim): Ascentry exhibited at an event in Dubai (UAE). — [Ascentry news/events](https://www.ascentry.com/case-studies/1ere-annee-dune-fusion-reussie-entre-byg-informatique-et-info-partner-partner4lab/)

### Inferences
- INDICATIVE: the middleware (Lab Composer and the E.O.M. workstations) is probably shipped as OEM or white-label with IVD analysers, which would explain the "4,500 labs" figure. This reach does not carry over to the Infection Tracker IPC module.

### Gaps
- UNKNOWN: Maghreb, francophone Africa, Asia-Pacific, Latin America and the DOM-TOM (web search only; no named customers or distributors found). Which other IVD OEM partners exist beyond QuidelOrtho is also unknown.
- UNKNOWN: purchasing frameworks usable across countries. None were found (French UGAP/UniHA frameworks were not checked; they are out of scope).
